the permission slip
"Collecting dead ideas isn't a sign of failure. It's proof you're creative, curious, and alive."

Most people have a graveyard of ideas they've never mentioned to anyone, notebooks half full, voice memos never replayed, ideas crossed out and rewritten on the next page. We think that graveyard is the most interesting thing about you. This isn't a business start-up resource, productivity advice, or a course that helps you find your one winning idea and execute it. It's a permission slip.

You might already belong here

Two kinds of people tend to find their way here, and both are welcome exactly as they are. No business plan required, no application, nothing to prove. If either of these feels familiar, hello, you're one of us.

the road less travelled

The Late Bloomers

  • You're in your 40s or 50s, and something in you wants more than the current status quo.
  • You spent decades parking creative ideas because you were told to be realistic.
  • You don't need any of it to become a business. You just want room to ask what it actually means to you.
the burst thinkers

The Late Diagnoses

  • You were diagnosed neurodivergent as an adult, or you suspect you might be, and it finally explained everything.
  • Your ideas arrive in bursts, not straight lines, and you've spent years feeling shame about that.
  • You're not lacking people. You're lacking somewhere to feel understood without performing a conversation to get there.
the four-stage system

Every idea gets a place to rest

Not every idea needs to become something. Some just need somewhere to be honoured. This is where every dead idea, living idea, or somewhere-in-between idea gets sorted, and it's the same framework The Dead Ideas Journal is built entirely around.

i.

The
Graveyard

Ideas that are fully laid to rest. Celebrated, honoured, released. Not a failure. A full life, lived.

ii.

Purgatory

Ideas that need more time, or more information, before a decision. No rush. They'll wait.

iii.

Resurrection

Ideas that are being actively pursued, right now, out here in the daylight.

iv.

Reincarnation

Ideas that need fundamental reworking before they can live again, in a new form.
